# Learn French with Aira

A comprehensible-input web app for learning French — leveled content from absolute beginner (A1) to native-level ("Pro", C2), with daily streaks to keep practice consistent.

## Method

Content is organized by CEFR level (A1–C2) and follows the comprehensible-input approach: read and listen to material that's slightly above your current level, look up what you don't know without stopping to drill it, and let vocabulary build through repeated, natural exposure.

See [`docs/PRD.md`](docs/PRD.md) for the full product requirements document.
